# Surface Brief: Tahes Core Application

## Scope & Mode
- **Scope:** Complete application surface (Feed, Outdoor GPS Tracker, Indoor Workout Player, Progress Hub, Settings)
- **Mode:** Operate
- **Audience:** 26yo, 165cm, 83kg user working toward 65kg with zero equipment and strict joint preservation.
- **Job:** Effortlessly track outdoor brisk walks/runs and indoor zero-impact bodyweight workouts without friction or physical confusion.
- **Constraints:** Zero equipment, knee protection first, high daylight legibility, mobile-first one-thumb reach, offline-first.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
The Tactical Rally Pacebook refuses the generic commercial pastel-card gym look. It treats fitness tracking as high-stakes co-driver stage notes: split intervals, clear safety limits, and bold tactile triggers.

### OWN-WORLD
Deep asphalt black ground (`#0b0f19`), high-visibility rally orange accents (`#ff3b00`), warning amber indicators (`#f59e0b`), joint shield emerald (`#10b981`), and stark white numeral matrices. Chamfered tactile buttons with heavy mechanical push feedback.

### STORY
The runner immediately grasps distance, instantaneous split pace, and elapsed duration under blinding sun. Indoors, they execute joint-safe sets guided by audible mechanical cues and high-visibility countdown meters.
