## Design Specificity Verdict

- **LLM Qualitative Assessment (8.0/10)**: Strong, disciplined break from generic commercial gym apps. Deep asphalt black (`#0B0F19`), technical borders (`#24334A`), Rally Orange (`#FF3B00`), and tabular monospaced numbers feel like an authoritative vehicle instrument panel. Residual category echoes include an unnecessary avatar bubble on offline activity cards and semantic overuse of the word "STAGE".
- **Deterministic Detector (100% Clean Pass)**: `impeccable detect` returned 0 anti-patterns across tokens, borders, contrasts, typography, and spacing. 41/41 unit tests pass.

## What's Working
1. **EMA Weight Smoothing & Behavioral Framing**: Separates daily water weight noise from true fat loss with clear explanatory guidance.
2. **High-Contrast Cockpit Tripmeter**: Ultra-readable 900-weight monospaced figures legible under direct outdoor tropical glare.
3. **Frictionless Offline Architecture**: 100% offline-first IndexedDB, synthetic Web Audio, no sign-in or tracking barriers.

## Priority Issues
- **[P0] Unprotected Critical Destructive Actions**: Single-tap finish in GPS tracker and 1-tap immediate activity deletion in feed.
- **[P1] "STAGE" Semantic Saturation**: The word "STAGE" is overloaded across 6 different UI layers.
- **[P2] In-Flight Joint Distress Regression CTA**: Missing hot-swap to regress exercises when experiencing knee twinges in workout player.
- **[P3] Inclusive Tab & Mode Nomenclature**: Tab 2 labeled "GPS Lari", alienating brisk walkers who cannot run at 83kg.
- **[P4] Remove Residual Social-Feed Artifacts**: Avatar initial circles in personal offline activity cards.

## Persona Red Flags
- **Maya (26yo, 83kg, Knee-Vulnerable)**: Knee feels sore at set 2 of squats; only options are Skip or Finish with no joint-relief regression alternative.
- **Alex (Brisk Walker)**: Bottom tab says "GPS Lari", creating imposter syndrome; accidental pocket touch stops tracking.
- **Jordan (Indoor Focus)**: Still sees "Rally Stage" motorsport terminology while doing living room rug calisthenics.
